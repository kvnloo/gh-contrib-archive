# Dream stills — v1 HUD vs v2 cinematic

Twelve stills from the v1 and v2 image-gen rounds, grouped by world.

**v1** stills are HUD product shots: fake GitHub chrome, search bars, issue cards, legends. Useful as interaction references, not as a place to match.

**v2** stills are *places*: camera, lights, materials, foreground / midground / background. Those are the dream-loop targets.

## Loop pick

**Mycelium cavern** (`v2-cinematic-cavern.png`). It is the strongest 3D-navigable volume: wet soil, a stream, volumetric teal hyphae, mushrooms, no HUD soup. Closest to what the live WebGL scene can become.

Runners-up (do not mix into the same loop):

- Phase hangar — two bead rings over a wet floor; second-best place.
- Metro street — wet Tokyo at ground level; HUD aerials are a different product.
