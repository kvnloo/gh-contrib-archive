# v1 target stills — experiment

These are the **first** image-gen stills used as dream-loop targets. They are weak (HUD soup, fake “World 1–5” chrome, not true in-engine). Keep them as a baseline; replace with handmade stills in `v2/` when you have them.

All five were generated at **16:9** with Cursor `GenerateImage`. Exact prompts:

## mycelium (`target-mycelium.png`)

Exact in-engine real-time 3D screenshot of a browser WebGL app, 16:9. Dark soil void. Bioluminescent teal-green mycelium hyphae growing from a dim center, branching toward glowing nutrient nodes labeled like tiny holographic GitHub issue orbs. Hyphae pulse with traveling light packets. Dense cluster of fused loops on the right (a large public OSS repo). Foggy unlabeled mist pockets on the left (private work, no readable names). Minimal HUD: top-left title MYCELIUM, five small world tabs. No concept art, not a painting, real Three.js game screenshot, sharp UI, 60fps look, volumetric fog, bloom.

## night / Black Marble (`target-night.png`)

Exact in-engine 3D screenshot of a WebGL night-earth visualization. Pitch black space, a curved dark planet surface filling the lower two thirds. City lights like NASA Black Marble: sparse harbors on the left (old years), a blazing megacity of cyan and amber points on the right (2025-2026). Tiny HUD overlay top: BLACK MARBLE, year scrubber. Hover tooltip on one light showing a public GitHub title. Dim anonymous glow patches with no labels for private events. Real-time renderer screenshot, not illustration, cinematic camera slightly tilted, bloom, film grain subtle.

## phase (`target-phase.png`)

Exact in-engine WebGL screenshot. Two giant concentric rings of glowing beads floating in a dark studio, like Steve Reich Piano Phase. Left ring cool silver (2016-2018 human era), right ring hot magenta-orange (2025-2026 agent era), slightly out of phase. Beads are issue/PR/comment glyphs. Blank darker beads on both rings for private events. Center title PHASE. Thin connecting lines between matching beat positions. Minimal chrome HUD with five world tabs. Photoreal real-time 3D, reflections on a black floor, not a diagram, not infographic, in-engine screenshot.

## metro (`target-metro.png`)

Exact in-engine 3D screenshot of a neon Tokyo metro map as a living 3D world. A glowing loop line (Yamanote-like) around the player camera, colored transit lines radiating to stations that are GitHub repo names in holographic type. NousResearch line dense and busy. Unlabeled dark tunnels with passenger-count numbers only (private orgs). Neon wet reflective floor, night city haze, trains as light streaks moving along lines. HUD: METRO. Real Three.js screenshot, not a flat transit poster.

## constellation (`target-constellation.png`)

Exact in-engine 3D screenshot of a starfield constellation. Thousands of stars, a dense nebula cluster labeled hermes-agent with named star-to-star links like issue citations. Isolated dust stars far away. Magenta nebula tint on agent-template stars. Camera in space, gentle parallax. HUD: CONSTELLATION, search box. Clicked star shows public issue title. No private repo names. Real-time WebGL, photographic space rendering, not a NASA photo, not a chart.
